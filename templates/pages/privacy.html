Demo notice: this page is a placeholder. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed for compliance with the DPDP Act, GDPR or any other regime.

Information collected

The enquiry form collects your name, company (optional), email address, phone number, product interest and message. A consent checkbox must be ticked before the form can be submitted.

Why it is collected

The information is used only to understand and respond to the parts enquiry. In this demonstration build, submissions are stored in a local SQLite database used for the client demo.

Who can see it

Enquiries are private. They are visible only to authenticated staff accounts through the admin panel at /admin/. They are not published on the public website and are excluded from search engine indexing.

How long it is kept

No retention schedule has been configured. Before launch, the business should define a retention period and remove enquiries that are no longer needed.

Your choices

Contact the business using the phone number or email address published on the contact page to request access to or deletion of an enquiry.

Cookies and tracking

This build sets a session cookie for staff login and a CSRF cookie for form protection. It does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics. The demonstration banner stores a dismissal preference in your browser's local storage.

Changes

This notice will be replaced with a reviewed version before the website is made public.
