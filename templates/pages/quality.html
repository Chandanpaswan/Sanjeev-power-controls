Inspection workflow

Five checkpoints before dispatch.

The sequence below is editable demo copy. Each step should be confirmed against the real process on site.

Proposed
  1. 01

    Requirement record

    Capture part number, engine model, dimensions, quantity and application.

  2. 02

    Incoming check

    Compare the component and its labelling with the agreed reference.

  3. 03

    Dimensional check

    Measure critical dimensions where the requirement calls for it.

  4. 04

    Fitment confirmation

    Re-check the application details before the order is released.

  5. 05

    Packing review

    Confirm quantity, labels and packing condition against the order.

Component verification

What gets checked, and what gets recorded.

A practical inspection record should make it obvious which checks were completed and which details were confirmed with the customer. The demo dashboard stores those details with every product record and enquiry.

  • Part number and reference match
  • Critical dimensions against the agreed specification
  • Visual condition and labelling
  • Quantity and packing contents
  • Customer confirmation for any unresolved fitment question
  • Enquiry reference retained for follow-up

The checklist is a proposed structure, not evidence of a certified quality management system.
