Who we are

DG set parts, presented with the details that matter.

What we do

Three product families, one enquiry path.

The demo catalogue is organised to mirror common DG set maintenance requirements.

Air, oil & fuel filtration

Intake elements, spin-on lubricant filters and fuel filter assemblies, each with a structured specification record.

Engine spare parts

Configurable service kits and individual engine components, with contents confirmed per enquiry.

Electrical components

Alternator-related components for generator applications, with ratings and mounting confirmed against the requirement.

How we work

A proposed enquiry workflow.

This sequence is demo positioning for the client to review and confirm — it is not a published service guarantee.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Share the part number, engine details, quantity and application.

  2. 02

    Verification

    Check the reference, dimensions and fitment assumptions with the customer.

  3. 03

    Quotation

    Issue commercial details only after the requirement is confirmed.

  4. 04

    Supply

    Prepare the agreed parts and packing against the order record.

  5. 05

    Follow-up

    Keep the enquiry reference available for repeat orders and support.
