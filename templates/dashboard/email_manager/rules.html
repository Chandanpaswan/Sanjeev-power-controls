Event reference

How each rule is triggered.

New enquiry
After the public enquiry form is validated and saved. The customer acknowledgement and the internal notification are separate rules.
Product enquiry
Used instead of the general internal notification when the enquiry names a product.
Lead assigned
Sent when a staff member is assigned to an enquiry in the dashboard.
Status changed
Sent when the enquiry status changes. The previous and new labels are included.
Follow-up due
Sent by python manage.py send_follow_up_reminders for open enquiries whose follow-up date has arrived. Schedule it with cron or your platform scheduler.
Password reset
Uses Django's secure, single-use reset token. The link is generated by Django and is never stored in a template.
