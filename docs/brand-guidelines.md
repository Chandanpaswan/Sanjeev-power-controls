# Brand Guidelines — Sanjeev Power Controls

## Brand name
Sanjeev Power Controls

## Industry
Diesel Generator (DG Set) parts manufacturing and power control components.

## Suggested tagline
DG Set Parts Manufacturer

## Suggested brand colors
- Navy Blue: `#0B1F3A` — trust, engineering, industrial strength
- Metallic Silver: `#BFC7D1` — machinery and precision
- Electric Orange: `#F28C28` — energy and emphasis
- White: `#FFFFFF` — clarity

## Logo direction
A clean SPC lettermark combined with a subtle gear or lightning motif. Keep the mark legible at small sizes and suitable for product labels, printed materials, uniforms, and the website.

## Logo files to add
- `assets/images/logo.png`
- `assets/images/logo-white.png`
- Prefer SVG for crisp scaling when available.

## Usage
Do not distort the logo or place it on a low-contrast background. Confirm trademark availability before investing in final brand assets.
